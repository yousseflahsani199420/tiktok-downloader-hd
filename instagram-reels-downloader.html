Copy a Reel link from Instagram

Open the Reel in the Instagram app, tap the Share control, and select Copy link. You can also copy the address of a public Reel while viewing it in a browser. Return to this page and paste the link without adding unrelated text. The processing service must be able to access the original post for a result to be returned.

Previewing a Reel before saving

When a video URL is returned, the page attempts to load it in the built-in browser video player. Some external media hosts restrict playback from other websites or expire links quickly. In those cases the preview may not play even though the download action can still work. The player’s availability depends on the media host, not only on the original Reel.
