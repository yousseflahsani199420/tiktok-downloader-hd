Frequently asked questions

How do I download an Instagram Reel?

Can I download a private Instagram post?

No. This page is intended for public links. It does not provide access to private accounts or content that requires a login.

Why does an Instagram link fail?

The post may be private, removed, unavailable in your region, or temporarily blocked from processing. Confirm that the link is public and correctly copied.

Can I choose audio only?
