SEO deployment notes — six-page build

1. Upload every file in this archive to the same website root. Do not upload the HTML pages into a subfolder.
2. Confirm the six canonical URLs and the sitemap at https://tiktokdownloader.online/sitemap.xml.
3. Submit or resubmit that sitemap in Google Search Console.
4. Inspect the three new guide URLs in Search Console after deployment; indexing and ranking are not guaranteed.
5. The new pages target distinct intents: TikTok MP4, Instagram Reels, and Facebook Reels. They keep the platform-specific downloader tool and add separate instructions, limitations, and FAQs.
6. Check the live pages with PageSpeed Insights and URL Inspection after publishing.

Do not create more near-identical landing pages just to multiply URLs. Add further pages only when they answer a different user need with substantial unique information or functionality.
